My Commission

William Griffin · Sales Rep Commission Plan

Your plan

  • Signing bonus: 100% of the client's first monthly fee
  • Deposit commission: 15% of the client's deposit
  • Monthly residual: 10% of the monthly fee for the first 12 months only. It stops after 12 months, or sooner if the client leaves
  • Build & add-on fees: 10% of any other one-time fees
  • Quarterly accelerator: sign 5+ new clients in a quarter and the residual rises to 15% on those deals
  • When you're paid: monthly, after the client's payment clears

90-day clawback

  • If a client cancels or doesn't pay within 90 days, the signing bonus and deposit commission come out of your next check
  • If you have no upcoming commissions, or you leave VersaNext, you repay it directly
  • Residuals already paid are never clawed back
  • These terms are part of your signed agreement
