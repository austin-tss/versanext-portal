How it's paid

  • Commissions are paid monthly, after the client's payment has been received.
  • Every payout is a percentage of what that client actually pays, so it scales with each deal.
  • If a client cancels or doesn't pay within their first 90 days, the signing bonus and deposit commission are taken back.
  • Sales reps sell only. After the sale, the Account Manager / Developer takes over the client.
  • Residuals stop when the client leaves or after 12 months, whichever comes first.

How the clawback works

  • The signing bonus and deposit commission are paid up front, right after the client's first payment.
  • If the client cancels or stops paying within 90 days, that amount is subtracted from the rep's next commission check.
  • Example: a rep earns $800 on a deal and the client quits in month two, so the rep's next payout is $800 lower.
  • If the rep has no upcoming commissions or leaves the company, they repay the amount directly.
  • Residuals already paid for months the client did pay are never clawed back.
  • These terms are part of each rep's signed commission agreement.
